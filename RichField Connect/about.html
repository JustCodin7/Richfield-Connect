About Richfield Connect

Learn more about our platform and what it stands for

What is Richfield Connect?

Richfield Connect is a secure, institution-focused online platform built exclusively for Richfield students. It gives you a safe space to create your academic profile, share ideas, and connect with fellow students across all campuses.

Our Mission

Our mission is to digitally transform student engagement by providing a structured and professional environment. Unlike commercial social media, Richfield Connect is aligned with institutional standards, professionalism, and data privacy.

What We Offer

Academic Profiles

Create and personalise your student profile with your details and interests.

Student Feed

Post updates, share ideas, and interact with posts from the community.

Privacy Focused

Your data stays within the Richfield network. No third party access.

All Campuses

Connect with students from every Richfield campus and online.
